# Part 12 — Exam Strategy

When reading a question:

```text
1. What is the REQUIREMENT?
2. What KEYWORD matters?
3. Which answers clearly do NOT fit?
4. What Azure service BEST satisfies the requirement?
```

Watch for wording such as:

- Best
- Most cost-effective
- Least administrative effort
- Highly available
- Secure
- Managed
- Serverless
- Relational
- NoSQL
- Private
- Compliant

Do not choose an answer only because you recognize the service name.

Choose it because it matches the requirement.
